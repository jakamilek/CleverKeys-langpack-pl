HerBERT FP32 benchmark package v1, 2026-10-04.

Original model: allegro/herbert-base-cased, revision
50e33e0567be0c0b313832314c586e3df0dc2297, CC BY 4.0.
Authors: Allegro Machine Learning Research Team and Linguistic Engineering Group,
Institute of Computer Science, Polish Academy of Sciences.
Model card: https://huggingface.co/allegro/herbert-base-cased
Paper: Mroczkowski, Wroblewska and Gawlik, HerBERT: Efficiently Pretrained
Transformer-based Language Model for Polish (2021):
https://aclanthology.org/2021.bsnlp-1.1/
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: converted masked-candidate mean/sum log-probability graph to FP32 ONNX.
No training and no quantization. Original fast tokenizer exported; derived portable
vocabulary/merge/Unicode tables preserve its behaviour and are tested separately.
No endorsement by original authors is implied. Preserve attribution and identify
conversion/table derivation as modifications with any redistribution of this package.

Tokenizer behaviour is based on Hugging Face tokenizers 0.22.2 (BertNormalizer,
BertPreTokenizer and cased Char-BPE) and transformers 4.57.6 HerbertTokenizerFast.
Those libraries are Apache-2.0; see https://github.com/huggingface/tokenizers and
https://github.com/huggingface/transformers. The portable interpreter is independently
implemented from exported tables and publicly documented algorithms.

Frozen source subset and diagnostic contexts are inherited from ai_metadata_v5 and
ai_compare_v5; see their PROTOCOL/NOTICE files. No full SGJP dictionary is included.
Benchmark only: no phone performance claim, production activation or release authority.
