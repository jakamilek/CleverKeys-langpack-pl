Glossary expands grammatical codes only; it is not a new lexical meaning database.
All lexical descriptions are generated from the existing lemma/POS/NAME/labels/forms.
Authored contexts and expected spellings are diagnostic annotations, not dictionary
attributes. New contexts were written after prior results and are not a blind corpus.
